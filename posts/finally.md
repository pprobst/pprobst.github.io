Last year I wrote a static blog generator called
[beatrice](https://github.com/pprobst/beatrice), with the
intent of applying my knowledge of Go (not the game; the programming language) and 
finally starting a kind-of-serious personal blog. It wasn't my first time trying to 
start a blog, but I planned it to be the _definitive_ one.

Naturally, I stopped programming in Go and my interest in blogging dwindled. 

Then I started thinking, "_huh, I guess starting a blog after
finishing university will be pretty interesting..._", but now, by impulse, I simply
started writing this post -- and right at the beginning of my penultimate semester
at the uni, thus betraying my promise with myself.

Therefore, regarding blogging, I simply won't keep promises anymore. My posts will
probably be quite erratic, but by writing my thoughts I'll be able to rationalize 
them a bit better. Hopefully.
